# The tree card

What the card of a tree shows when it is clicked: title and data, photo, errors and hints, the editors and "Fix with JOSM", and the two drawings to scale of the trunk.

## Title and data

* The card title is the tree's name (`name`); if missing, the common name of the species in the site's language (e.g. "Judas tree" for *Cercis siliquastrum*); if that is missing too, the species in italics. The value used as the title is not repeated in the data below. The common name comes from Wikidata: the item is that of `species:wikidata` if correct, otherwise that of the species in the wiki list; the label is used if it is not the scientific name, otherwise the first alias that is not (in Italian the labels of plants are almost always the scientific name). A wrong common name is therefore fixed on Wikidata, by changing the label or the order of the aliases. With `wikidata=*`, the card also has the link to the tree's Wikipedia article, if it exists.
* Clicking a tree opens the card with the main data (a tree with `diameter=*` but no `circumference=*` shows a "Circumference" row with the diameter multiplied by π, marked "(from diameter)"; `diameter` without a unit is in millimetres; with the browser in `en-US` and the site in English, the "Height", "Circumference" and "Crown diameter" rows are in feet and inches, e.g. "65 ft 7 in", with the value in metres on hover, while the messages of the checks stay in metres; the "Planting date" row, from `start_date=*`, is followed by the age in brackets, [issue #61](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/61): today minus the date, in completed years with a full date, the difference of the years with the year only, preceded by "~" for `~`, decades and centuries, ">" for `before`, "<" for `after`, two ages for a range such as `1500..1600`, nothing for the forms not read by Oldest in Record trees or for dates in the future), a "Multi-stemmed" row with `multi_stemmed=*` ([issue #46](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/46): `yes` → "yes", `3` → "3 trunks", `1;1.5;3` → "3 trunks with circumference 1 m, 1.5 m and 3 m", any other value as written), the problems found and the links to Wikidata, Commons, Panoramax and Fotoshi (Commons and Fotoshi only if the photo shown is not already that one: in that case the photo credit links there). In both maps, the species (the "Species" row, or the title when the species is the title) is a link that opens, in a new tab, the page of the tree's species in `species/` (records, typical sizes, where it is mapped), when that species has one: the species is that of `species=*` (whitespace and case aside) or, for a synonym, the name in the wiki list. Trees with more than one species (`;`) have no link. The pages that exist are listed in `species/index.json` and shown in `species/index.html` (sorted by number of trees by default, or by name; author and licence of each photo under it, from Wikimedia Commons), which opens from "Species" at the top of each species page and from the button next to the genus/species menu ([Map](mappa.md)). The "Edit ▾" entry opens a menu with the node's page on OpenStreetMap and the editors to fix it: iD (the OSM web editor), JOSM (loads the area around the tree into the already open editor and selects it, if remote control is on) and MapComplete (trees theme). With iD and JOSM the changeset gets `hashtags=#dendrosm` and as `source` the site address with the open region (e.g. `https://ivanbranco.github.io/tree-osm-qa/#region=slovenia`).
* After the "Species" row the card has a "Variety" row and a "Cultivar" row ([issue #68](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/68)). The variety is the value of `taxon:variety=*` or, if missing, of `variety=*`; the cultivar that of `taxon:cultivar=*` or, if missing, of `cultivar=*`. Without those tags they are read from `species=*`: the epithet after `var.` (`species=Quercus robur var. fastigiata` → Species "Quercus robur", Variety "fastigiata") and the cultivar in quotation marks, shown without them (`species=Acer palmatum 'Atropurpureum'` → Species "Acer palmatum", Cultivar "Atropurpureum"); the "Species" row then shows the name without them. A cultivar without quotation marks is read too, after a species of the wiki list (`species=Pyrus calleryana Chanticleer` → Species "Pyrus calleryana", Cultivar "Chanticleer"); an abbreviated genus is shown in full (`species=T. baccata 'Fastigiata'` with `genus=Taxus` → Species "Taxus baccata"); with a genus and only the cultivar (`species=Magnolia 'Susan'`) the card has a "Genus" row instead of "Species". When the species is the card title, the title stays the whole value. The reading from `species` arrives with the next update of the region (properties `sp_base`, `sp_gen`, `sp_var`, `sp_cv`, `sp_abbr`), under the conditions of the [hint](check.md#hints) that moves them to their own tags
* With `sex=male` or `sex=female` (dioecious plants, such as ginkgo or yew) ♂ or ♀ appears next to the card title, in grey; other values are not shown. Clicking it opens a box with "Male specimen" or "Female specimen" and, for some dioecious species and genera (Ginkgo biloba, Taxus baccata, Ilex aquifolium, Laurus nobilis, Juniperus communis, Acer negundo, Ceratonia siliqua, Ailanthus altissima, Araucaria araucana, Diospyros lotus, Diospyros virginiana, Juniperus oxycedrus, Juniperus virginiana, Phytolacca dioica, Schinus molle, Populus nigra, and the genera Populus, Salix, Phoenix, Broussonetia, Cephalotaxus, Cercidiphyllum, Chamaerops, Gymnocladus, Hippophae, Maclura, Phellodendron, Pistacia, Podocarpus, Torreya, Trachycarpus; a species followed by a variety or cultivar counts as that species), the traits that tell males and females apart; for Ginkgo biloba also a photo from Wikimedia Commons (seeds or male cones). The species pages (`species/`) of those species and genera show the same traits and photos in a "Male and female" section after "On the map": the male on the left and the female on the right, each with the number of trees of the species tagged `sex=male` or `sex=female` in the site's regions
* A tree of a dioecious species or genus (the same list as the "Sex" row of the Completeness tab) without `sex=*` gets ♂/♀ next to the title instead, in a lighter grey. Clicking it opens a box saying that the species is dioecious and the sex of the tree is not given, followed by a link to the "Male and female" section of the species page if the species has one in `species/`, otherwise by the traits of both sexes when they are known (the list above). Trees with several species (`species=a;b`) and trees with another value of `sex=*` get no symbol
* If present, the card shows `ref:masaf` (MASAF list of monumental trees of Italy) with the monumentality criteria from `masaf:criteria` (only the names, without the codes: for example `a2.1,a2.2,g` becomes "circumference, height, historical-cultural or religious value", see the [wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/IT:Key:ref:masaf#Criteri_di_monumentalit%C3%A0)), `ref:rami` (RAMI register), `heritage:operator=icnf` (tree of public interest protected by the ICNF in Portugal, with the code from `ref:icnf` if present), `heritage:operator=anf` (tree protected by the ANF in Luxembourg), `ref:CRFOP` or `ref:gid` (natural monument in the Polish CRFOP register, Centralny Rejestr Form Ochrony Przyrody: one line, with the code from `ref:CRFOP`, or from `ref:gid` if `ref:CRFOP` is missing) and `website`/`url` pointing to monumentaltrees.com. The lines of the official lists and registers (MASAF, RAMI, ICNF, ANF, CRFOP) start with a ★; the MASAF criteria are indented under the MASAF line.
* A tree that is a member of a relation with `ref:masaf` (a [homogeneous group](mappa.md#homogeneous-groups-of-the-masaf-list) of the MASAF list) has, in both maps, a ★ line "Part of a homogeneous group listed in the MASAF list of monumental trees of Italy (code), “name of the relation” · relation on OpenStreetMap", with the `ref:masaf` of the relation, its `name` if it has one and the link to the relation; below it, indented, the criteria of the relation's `masaf:criteria`, if any (only the names, as for trees). One line per relation, if the tree is a member of more than one. If the tree also has a `ref:masaf` of its own, its MASAF line stays and the group line is added. If the MASAF list gives a code of the relation as removed, the line reads "Part of a homogeneous group formerly listed…" and the tree is still drawn as monumental. On the monumental trees map these trees do not have the note for trees monumental according to other sources ("Not marked as monumental on OSM, but…").
* A tree in a Chilean region (`iso` CL in `scripts/regions.json`; on the monumental trees map, the QA region the tree falls in) whose `species` is one of the seven species declared a Natural Monument in Chile has, in both maps, a ★ line "Species declared a Natural Monument in Chile (decree n of year)", with a link to the decree on the Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional: *Araucaria araucana* (decree 43 of 1990), *Fitzroya cupressoides* (decree 490 of 1976), *Gomortega keule*, *Pitavia punctata*, *Beilschmiedia berteroana*, *Beilschmiedia miersii* and *Nothofagus alessandrii* (decree 13 of 1995). Each decree applies to every living individual of the species in the national territory; stumps get no line. The line depends only on the species and the region, not on the tree's tags: these trees are not drawn as monumental and are not added to the monumental trees map (a monumental tree is not necessarily a Natural Monument, and the other way round, [issue #82](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/82)). The list is `CL_MONUMENT_SPECIES` in `index.html`.
* The mouse wheel over a card zooms the map, as it does outside the card

## Photo

The image comes, in order, from `wikimedia_commons=File:…`, `panoramax`, `image:fotoshi` ([Fotoshi](https://www.fotoshi.com/), with the link to the photo and, before the author's name in the credit, the Fotoshi symbol, as on osm.fotoshi.com), `image` (an address, or a Commons file written as `image=File:…`, shown like `wikimedia_commons`; for a Google Photos link the photo read by the update from the shared page, `data/gphotos.json`, with "Google Photos" and the link to the page in place of the credit) and from the image (P18) of the Wikidata item of `wikidata=*`, if it is not a taxon. When the photo comes from Wikidata and the item also has a winter view (P5252), the card shows the winter view from December to March (June to September south of the equator), or with `season=bare` in the address. If there is only `wikimedia_commons=Category:…`, the card shows the first file of the category, requested from Commons when it opens. Images that fail to load are hidden. At the bottom right of the photo, author and licence appear, with the link to the source, once the photo is loaded; if they do not fit on one line, only the author is shortened (with "…", full text on hover) and the licence stays visible: for Commons files they are requested from the Commons API when the card opens, for Panoramax from the Panoramax API, for Fotoshi the script reads them from the photo page. For Commons the author is the attribution required in the licence template (`|attribution=`), if present; otherwise the author field, without the parts hidden by templates, and if this contains links (for example a box with name and contacts) only the text of the first link. For Panoramax the author is the one indicated in the photo, if any (among the "providers", the one without an account, from the EXIF data), otherwise the account that uploaded it (for example `mapcomplete`). The "Panoramax" link at the bottom of the card does not appear if the photo shown is already that one.

## Errors and hints

* On the card each error is preceded by ⚠: hovering over it with the mouse (or tapping it on a phone) shows the explanation of the check, for example where the threshold comes from. The texts are in `why` in the `I18N` object of `index.html`, one per error code
* After the errors (⚠, in red) the card can show hints (ⓘ, in grey): information that could improve the data, not errors; see [Hints](check.md#hints)

## Fix with JOSM

"Fix with JOSM" ([issue #19](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/19)): when the card has errors or hints with only one possible fix, the "Edit ▾" menu shows "Fix with JOSM" instead of "Open in JOSM", preceded by ⚠ if it fixes errors, ⓘ if it applies hints, or both. The link works like "Open in JOSM" (same changeset hashtag and source) and also passes the tags to change (`addtags`) and a changeset comment in English (e.g. "Remove the author name from species; add leaf_type"). JOSM downloads the tree as it is now and shows the tags before applying them, so the user checks them and can cancel. Hovering the entry lists the changes (`species: Thuya occidentalis L. → Thuya occidentalis`, or `leaf_type=broadleaved` for an added tag); the old values are those of the last update of the region. Errors with a fix: "Species with the author name" (the name without the author, or the name proposed by "Species possibly misspelled" if the name also has a spelling error), "Species possibly misspelled" (the proposed name, only when `species` is genus and epithet, also with the author, and nothing else), both with the missing `species:wikidata`, `leaf_type` and `leaf_cycle` of the corrected species (only with data from the region update after this change), "Species with wrong capitalisation" (the name with only the initial of the genus uppercase), "Species starting lowercase" and "Genus starting lowercase" (capital first letter; not for a genus of more than one word), "Non-scientific genus" (the scientific name: `genus: wierzba → Salix`), "Genus differs from species" when `genus` looks like the genus of `species` misspelled (`genus: Styphlonobium → Styphnolobium`), "Plant that is not a tree" (`natural: tree → plant`), "Single-word species" when the word is a genus or the common name of a genus (`species=spruce` is removed and `genus=Picea` is added if `genus` is missing; not with a different `genus`), "Decimal comma" (only for a number with one comma between the digits, e.g. `2,9 m` → `2.9 m`; not with exactly three digits after the comma, `1,200`, which can mean 1.2 or 1200), "Unit written in a non-standard form" (the standard form; for metres, which have two, the one without a unit: `circumference: 4.8m → 4.8`, and `diameter: 2 M → 2000`, in millimetres), "Rare or invalid denotation" when a value has spaces around the `;` (`denotation: urban; natural_monument → urban;natural_monument`; only with data from the region update after this change) and "species:xx without species" for a common name of a genus or of a species (see [check.md](check.md)): `species:pl=Dąb` is removed and becomes `genus:pl=Dąb`, plus `genus=Quercus` if `genus` is missing (only if all the names give the same genus and no `genus:xx` has another name; if `genus:xx` already has that name `species:xx` is only removed; hovering shows `species:pl: Dąb → (removed)`; only with data from the region update after this change), a genus with a single species or the name of a species adds `species=*` (not with a different `genus`) and, when a single species is proposed, the missing tags that the reference list gives for it: `species:wikidata`, `leaf_type` and `leaf_cycle`, not `genus`, which is implicit in `species` (`species:it=Bagolaro` → `species=Celtis australis`, `species:wikidata=Q255375`, `leaf_type=broadleaved`, `leaf_cycle=deciduous`; only with data from the region update after this change); if the name of a species is written with a different capitalization than in `species_names`, `species:xx` is rewritten as in the list (`species:en: northern red oak → Northern Red Oak`, only with data from the region update after this change), "taxon:xx without taxon" for a common name of a genus or of a species, or any name when `species` is present (see [check.md](check.md)): `taxon:xx` is removed and becomes `genus:xx` or `species:xx`, plus `genus` or `species` if missing and, for an added species, its missing `species:wikidata`, `leaf_type` and `leaf_cycle` (`taxon:it=pino` → `genus:it=Pino`, `genus=Pinus`; only with data from the region update after this change), "Wikipedia article in website" when `wikipedia=*` already has the same article (`website` or `url` is removed). Hints with a fix: missing `leaf_type` and `leaf_cycle`, `species` (from `species:wikidata`, from `species:wikipedia` or for a genus with one living species), `species:wikidata`, `masaf:criteria`, `species:it` and `name:en` with a capital letter, `taxon` moved to `species` or `genus` when the wiki lists know the name, or to `species` when it is a known genus followed by an epithet (`taxon` is removed), the hybrid sign `×` in place of the letter `x` in `species` and `genus` (`species: Platanus x hispanica → Platanus × hispanica`), feet and inches without spaces, the zero before the point of a number (`circumference: .75 → 0.75`), `wikimedia_commons` in place of an `image` pointing to a Commons file, also `image=File:…` (`image` is removed; only with a single value), `mapillary=<id>` in place of an `image` that is the page of a Mapillary photo (`image` is removed; only with data from the region update after this change). No fix for checks and hints that need a choice (a common name, an underscore, `denotation` for a tree with Wikidata) or a survey (the species from the tree's Wikidata item). If two fixes give different values for the same tag, that tag is left out; values containing `|` (the tag separator in the JOSM link), or `;` in `species` and `genus`, are left out too. Stumps get only the feet-and-inches, zero-before-the-point, `wikimedia_commons` and `mapillary` fixes among the hints, as on the card. Nothing on the monumental trees map, and nothing in iD, which cannot receive tag changes through its address.

### All the trees in view

The **J** key ([issue #77](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/77), for now only from the keyboard, without a button) passes to JOSM the fixes of all the trees in view that have one, each with its own tags: the same fixes as "Fix with JOSM", only from zoom 17 and at most 25 trees at a time (the first 25 if there are more; in [expert mode](mappa.md#keyboard-shortcuts), key E, from zoom 10 and at most 100), counting only the trees shown with the filters chosen in the menus. Before sending, the page lists the trees and their changes and asks for confirmation. JOSM first downloads the area in view, then receives one request per tree (the tree selected, with its tags to change) and shows the confirmations one at a time, so each tree is checked and can be cancelled on its own. The changeset comment joins the reasons of all the fixes, with the same hashtag and source as "Fix with JOSM". The first time, the browser may ask permission to access the local network (JOSM listens on `127.0.0.1`); without it, or if JOSM is not open with remote control enabled, the page says that JOSM does not respond. If no tree in view has a fix, the page says so and offers to download the area in view in JOSM anyway (with the same hashtag and source). Nothing on the monumental trees map.

## Trunk comparison

Next to the height, a small icon (two trunks and a person) opens the "Trunk comparison" window; the **C** key does the same. For trees with one of the errors on the height/circumference ratio ("tree too slender", "tree too slender for its species", "tree too stocky", see [Measurements](check.md#measurements)) it is the window of that error ([issue #14](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/14), [issue #62](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/62)); the other trees get it if they have a mapped height and circumference, see [Trees without errors](#trees-without-errors). The window is closed with Esc, with the × button or by clicking outside it. While it is open the address contains `compare=side` (`#region=<slug>&node=<id>&compare=side`): the link opens the card with the window already open, for example to show a reported value to the mapper in a changeset comment ([issue #83](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/83), [Address parameters](mappa.md#address-parameters)).

### The drawing

The window shows a schematic drawing, to scale, of the trunk (as tall as `height`, as wide as the diameter, i.e. the circumference divided by π) next to the typical trunk of a tree of the same height, a 1.75 m person and, for trees taller than 5 m, a 7 m two-storey house. For trees taller than 40 m the person is replaced by the Leaning Tower of Pisa (56 m tall, 15.5 m wide at the base, leaning 4°), so the references are the house and the tower. When the trunk diameter is above 5 m (`CMP_BACK_FROM` in `index.html`) a reference for the width is drawn behind the trunk, in side view, on the same ground line and centred on the trunk: a sauropod (the size of Giraffatitan, about 22 m long and 12 m tall), a Boeing 747 (68.6 m long, 19.6 m tall) or the Colosseum (189 m long, 48 m tall, seen along its major axis). The smallest one of which the trunk is at most half of the length is used (`CMP_BACK_NEXT`), otherwise the Colosseum: the sauropod up to a diameter of 11 m, the Boeing 747 up to 34.3 m, the Colosseum above. With one of these the person is not drawn, and a sentence states the trunk diameter as a share of the length of the reference. The Colosseum is drawn in front of the trunk when the trunk would cover more than three quarters of it (share of the length times share of the height) and the Colosseum, in front, would cover less of the trunk: a trunk as wide as the Colosseum but lower stays in front. The person and the house are left out when they would be less than 7 px tall at the scale of the drawing, and the line of the references lists only the ones that are drawn. The typical trunk uses the species ratio (`height_circ_ratio`) for "tree too slender for its species" and the median of all OSM data with both measurements mapped (12: 11.8 on 368,958 trees of the regions in November 2026, `TYPICAL_HC_RATIO` in `index.html`, the same value as `_default` in `scripts/species_limits.json`) for the other two. A trunk thinner than 1 px at the scale of the drawing is drawn 1 px wide and this is stated below the drawing. The trunk has the same colour as on the map ([issue #47](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/47)): brown, white with a thin grey outline for *Betula utilis*, red-orange for *Acer griseum*, rainbow bands for *Eucalyptus deglupta*. The typical trunk takes the colour of the species it stands for (brown when it is the median of all trees), and the colour square in the captions follows the trunk. The trunk is drawn slightly narrower at the top than at the base ([issue #43](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/43)): the base is as wide as the measured diameter and the top is 15% narrower (at least 2 px); for stocky trunks less, down to 5% when the trunk is at least 0.6 times as wide as it is tall, and a trunk drawn less than 4 px wide stays a rectangle. The typical trunk is drawn in the same way. The drawing is schematic: the height includes the crown and the narrowing of the trunk towards the top is only indicative

### Trees without errors

The same window is also available for trees without these errors. Only trees that have a mapped `height` and a mapped circumference (`circumference=*` or from `diameter=*`) get it; stumps and trees with an estimated trunk are excluded. A `diameter=*` without unit below 10 (the "diameter_unit" error) is not used to draw the trunk on the map, but the comparison draws it as it is, in millimetres (the default unit of `diameter=*`), to show the reported value. With a species that has `height_circ_ratio` the drawing includes the typical trunk of the species; otherwise (no species, or a species without a ratio) it shows only the tree to scale with the references (person, house, Leaning Tower of Pisa, sauropod, Boeing 747, Colosseum): there is no fallback to the median of all data or to the genus. The texts only describe the sizes and how many times the trunk diameter is the typical one ("as wide as the typical one" within 10%): they do not say that a measurement is wrong. For trees with one of the three errors above the icon and the C key open the window of the error, described above. The data for the typical trunk (`hc_ratio`, `hc_circ`, `sp_ratio`) is written by `to_feature` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`, so each region gets that comparison at its next update; the drawing without a typical trunk uses `height` and the radius of the drawn trunk (`tr`), already in the data

## Trunk from above

Next to the circumference (also when it comes from `diameter`), a small icon with two concentric circles opens "Trunk from above" ([issue #60](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/60)): a scale drawing of the trunk seen from above, as a circle with the mapped circumference. If the tree has a height and its species has a known height/circumference ratio, the typical trunk of the species at the same height is drawn as a dashed circle on the same centre, and the text gives how many times wider the trunk is. Next to the circles, at the same scale and upright (its length from top to bottom, so that it can be compared with the diameter), a reference seen from above: the shoulders of a person, a car (4.5 × 1.8 m), a city bus (12 × 2.55 m), a tennis court (23.77 × 10.97 m), a football pitch (105 × 68 m) or the Colosseum (189 × 156 m), whichever has the length closest to the trunk diameter; a sentence below compares the diameter with that length (for example "about half the length of a car"). Not shown for stumps. While the window is open the address contains `compare=top`, so the link opens the card with this window ([issue #83](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/83))
