# QA checks

The checks DendrOSM runs on every tree, with their thresholds and where they come from.

## QA checks

Trees with at least one error have a red outline. The checks are:

* Circumference greater than 43 m: the largest recorded, in African baobabs (*Adansonia digitata*), according to [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/living-tree-with-greatest-girth)
* Height greater than 116.22 m: the tallest measured tree, [Hyperion](https://www.monumentaltrees.com/en/usa/california/delnortecounty/4462_redwoodnationalpark/9104/) (*Sequoia sempervirens*, Redwood National Park, California)
* `diameter_crown=*` greater than 60.4 m: the widest crown of a living single-trunk tree, a [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/635581-widest-crown-on-a-living-tree-single-trunk) record (*Samanea saman* "Chamchuri", Thailand, 2018). Banyans (*Ficus benghalensis*), which have many trunks, can exceed the threshold
* `tree=*` or `type=*`, tags not documented for trees: check whether they can be replaced with another tag. `type=palm` is not reported, because it needs to be fixed in bulk
* Circumference and height greater than the record of the single species. The records are documented on the wiki page [Tree species records](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:natural%3Dtree/Tree_species_records) and come from monumentaltrees.com, except the height of *Celtis australis* (from MASAF). The limits are copied into `scripts/species_limits.json`, where `height_source` or `circumference_source` indicate a source other than monumentaltrees.com, shown on the card; the comparison with `species=*` is exact (no variants or cultivars) and applies only if the generic check has not already been triggered
* Exaggerated height/circumference ratio: "tree too slender" if `height` is more than 100 times the circumference (e.g. `height=24`, `circumference=0.01`), "tree too stocky" if the circumference is more than 3 times `height`. The thresholds are deliberately loose, to avoid false positives: the stockiest real trees, such as monumental olive trees, are around 0.5. It does not apply if the circumference (for stocky) or the height (for slender) is already reported as over the limits, nor to stumps. The thresholds are `SLENDER_MAX` and `STOCKY_MIN` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`
* Tree too slender for its species: for species with a typical height/circumference ratio (`height_circ_ratio` in `scripts/species_limits.json`, the median of the OSM data on the wiki page [Tree species records](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:natural%3Dtree/Tree_species_records)), the tree's ratio exceeds 4 times that value and in any case 40. It applies only to trees at least 10 m tall, because newly planted saplings are thin; it does not apply if the height is already reported or if the generic check already applies. Tested on 59,169 trees, it reported 81. The thresholds are `SLENDER_SP_FACTOR`, `SLENDER_SP_MIN` and `SLENDER_SP_MIN_H` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`
* `species=*` possibly misspelled: it is not in the species list, but is very close to it (e.g. `Olea europeea` → `Olea europaea`). Genus and epithet are compared, without the hybrid sign `×` and without cultivar or author: with a one-letter difference it is always reported, with two only if the written genus is not a known genus (so `Myrtus communis` does not become `Pyrus communis`). True species that are not in the list but look similar are excluded in `scripts/whitelist.json` (`species_typo`)
* Species name in a language (`species:it=*`, `species:pl=*`, `species:en=*`…) without `species=*`, i.e. without the scientific name. 2-3 letter language codes are valid, also with a variant (`species:zh-Hans`); `species:wikidata` and `species:wikipedia` do not count
* `species=*` with an uppercase epithet (`Catalpa Bignonioides`, `Taxus Baccata`): the name with a lowercase epithet is suggested, the one from the species list or, for two-word names with a known genus, the same word in lowercase and the genus with only the initial capitalised (`PRUNUS COMUNIS` → `Prunus comunis`)
* `species=*` that does not have the form of a scientific name (Genus epithet) because the second word is uppercase: common names (`Pino Marittimo`, `Olive Tree`, `Black Matipo`) or cultivars without quotes (`Malus Golden Hornet`). If the scientific name is written in parentheses (`Black Matipo (Pittosporum tenuifolium)`) the card suggests it. Cultivars in quotes and authors do not count
* Parentheses in `species=*`: no scientific name contains them. Not repeated if the parentheses are already explained by the checks on the author or on the non-scientific name
* `species=*` indicating an unidentified species (`Pinus sp.`, `Pinus spp`): `genus=Pinus` without `species` should be used
* `species=*` with the author of the scientific name (`Phoenix dactylifera L.`, `Camellia sinensis (L.) Kuntze`, `Picea abies (L.) H. Karst.`): the card suggests the name without the author, keeping infraspecific ranks (`var.`, `subsp.`…) and cultivars in quotes. It applies only if the second word is lowercase, as in every scientific name, so as not to mistake common names for authors (`Black Matipo (Pittosporum tenuifolium)`)
* Underscore (`_`) in `species=*` or `genus=*` instead of a space
* Tag written inside a value: `=` in `species`, `genus`, `taxon`, `name`, `start_date`, `ref:masaf` or `ref:rami` (e.g. `genus=leaf_type=broadleaved`). Links (`image`, `website`, `url`) are not checked, because `=` is normal in addresses. With `=` in `species` or `genus` the checks on the form of the name are not repeated, since they would be triggered by chance
* `species=*` or `genus=*` in a non-Latin script (e.g. `genus=череша`): scientific names are written in the Latin script, the name in the local language goes in `species:xx`. Accented Latin letters are not reported. As with `=`, the checks on the form of the name are not repeated on that tag and `genus` is not compared with `species`
* `species=*` made of a single word
* `genus=*` made of more than one word. The leading hybrid sign does not count: `× Sycoparrotia` (intergeneric hybrid genus) is a single word, and it does not count for the capital letter and for the comparison with `species` either
* `species=*` or `genus=*` starting with a lowercase letter (for `species` only with more than one word: with a single word the previous error is enough)
* `circumference=*`, `height=*`, `diameter_crown=*` or `diameter=*` with a comma as decimal separator (`2,9` instead of `2.9`)
* `diameter=*` without a unit and less than 10 (e.g. `diameter=0.5`): without a unit the [wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:diameter) indicates millimetres, and it would be a trunk under 1 cm. If the value is in metres the unit must be written (`diameter=0.5 m`). These values are not used for the circumference; the check applies even if `circumference` is present. The threshold is `DIAM_MM_MIN` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`
* Trees at the same point: two or more `natural=tree` nodes with exactly the same coordinates (stumps do not count). The card lists the other nodes
* Overlapping trunks: the distance between two trees is less than the sum of the radii of their trunks. Only measured trunks count (from `circumference` or `diameter`, not estimated ones) that are not already reported as too large; a tree without a measured trunk counts as a point, and is reported (with the other) if it falls inside the trunk of another tree. Stumps and trees at the same point do not count. If they are the trunks of a single tree, only one node with `multi_stemmed=*` should be mapped. These two checks compare nearby trees, so they are computed after the single-tree ones (`neighbour_checks` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`)
* `circumference=*` and `diameter=*` both present but not matching: the circumference differs by more than 20% from π × diameter (with `diameter` without a unit in millimetres). The margin lets measurement inaccuracies through, such as non-round trunks. The threshold is `CIRC_DIAM_TOL` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`
* `circumference=*`, `height=*`, `diameter_crown=*` or `diameter=*` with text, ranges, lists or values of 0 (or negative); values with units (`150 cm`, `12 ft`) are not reported
* `genus=*` different from the first word of `species=*` (the leading hybrid sign `×` is ignored); not checked if `species` is a single lowercase word (e.g. `species=alba`), which is not a genus
* Links with an invalid format: `wikidata` other than `Q…`, `wikimedia_commons` without `File:` or `Category:`, `panoramax` that is not a UUID, `image` that does not start with `http`
* Unreachable links in `image`, `website` and `url` (nonexistent page, 404 or 410, or a site that no longer exists) and `wikimedia_commons=File:…` files that do not exist on Commons. 403, 429, server errors and timeouts are not reported, as they are often blocks against automated tools. Links on the same site are checked one at a time, and each link is rechecked at most every 30 days (`URL_RECHECK_DAYS`)
* `ref:masaf`, `ref:rami`, `ref:monumentaltrees`, `heritage:operator=icnf` (Portugal) or `heritage:operator=anf` (Luxembourg), `website`/`url` pointing to monumentaltrees.com, or `wikidata=*` whose item has the monumentaltrees.com ID ([P9704](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P9704)) or is a direct instance (P31) of monumental plant ([Q811534](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q811534)), without `denotation=natural_monument` (a monumental tree without a source is not reported). P9704 and P31 are read from Wikidata at every update, together with the other data of the item
* `ref:masaf=*` of a tree that the MASAF list of monumental trees of Italy gives as removed (due to natural death, felling, loss of requirements or downgrading): the card gives the reason, and the recognition row becomes "Formerly listed in the MASAF list of monumental trees of Italy…". It does not apply to stumps (`natural=tree_stump`), for which the code of a removed tree is normal. Codes are compared without spaces and ignoring leading zeros (`03/G114/CN/01` = `003/G114/CN/01`). The list is in `scripts/masaf_list.json`, produced with `scripts/update_masaf.py` from the KML file that MASAF publishes on the [list page](https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/11260); source: Masaf - Direzione generale dell'economia montana e delle foreste - Difor4 - "dataset AMI - Censimento alberi monumentali d'Italia vs. 11_2024", licence CC BY 4.0
* `masaf:criteria=*` with invalid codes: only `a`–`g`, `a1`, `a2`, `a2.1`, `a2.2`, `a2.3` are allowed, separated by commas without spaces (see the [wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/IT:Key:ref:masaf#Criteri_di_monumentalit%C3%A0)); spaces, uppercase, `;` and empty parts are also reported
* `species:wikidata=*` inconsistent with `species=*` according to the [OSM wiki species list](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:natural%3Dtree/List_of_Species): a QID different from the one expected for the species, or a QID that belongs to another species. It is not reported if on Wikidata the scientific name (P225) or the basionym ([P566](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P566)) of the item is exactly `species` (the list may use the other name: for example `Mespilus germanica` and `Crataegus germanica`). Other accepted QIDs (for example synonyms) are added, per species, in `scripts/whitelist.json`
* `species:wikidata=*` or `genus:wikidata=*` that on Wikidata are not a taxon, i.e. do not have a scientific name ([P225](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P225)), for example an illustration, a fruit or a single monumental tree, or nonexistent items. If it triggers on `species:wikidata`, the previous check on the same key is not repeated
* `wikidata=*` that is a taxon: `wikidata` indicates the individual tree, the species goes in `species:wikidata`
* `species:wikidata=*` that on Wikidata has the rank of genus or higher (section, family…), or `genus:wikidata=*` with the rank of species or lower (property [P105](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P105))
* Scientific name on Wikidata ([P225](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P225)) of the item of `species:wikidata=*` or `genus:wikidata=*` different from `species=*` or `genus=*`. It applies to all species, not only those in the wiki list; it is not repeated if the same error is already reported by the list. Genus and epithet are compared, without `×`; the item's basionym (P566) and the QID that the wiki list associates with the species are also accepted; the QIDs in `whitelist.json` are accepted
* Species of the tree on Wikidata (property [P10241](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P10241), "individual of taxon", of the item of `wikidata=*`) different from `species:wikidata=*` and from `species=*`
* Protected tree on Wikidata (property [P1435](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P1435)) without `denotation=natural_monument`
* `wikidata=*` whose Wikidata item has coordinates (P625) more than 1 km from the tree (if it has more than one, the nearest counts; items without coordinates or that are a taxon are not checked). The threshold is `WD_MAX_DIST` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`
* `wikidata=*` whose item has no coordinates, but has an image (P18) with coordinates on Commons more than 500 m from the tree (the subject's position if the file gives one, otherwise the camera position; same `COMMONS_MAX_DIST` threshold as the check on Commons photos). If the same photo is also linked to the tree (`wikimedia_commons` or `image`), only that check examines it
* Linked Commons files (`wikimedia_commons=File:…`, or `image=*` pointing to a file page on Commons) whose coordinates are more than 500 m from the tree. The subject's position (*object location*) is used if the file gives one, otherwise the camera position (*camera location*); files without coordinates are not checked. Coordinates are read from the Commons API and re-read at most every 30 days, like links. The threshold is `COMMONS_MAX_DIST` in `scripts/fetch_trees.py`
* `leaf_type=*` or `leaf_cycle=*` (valid) different from those of the species in the species list or, if the species is not there, of the genus in the [genus list](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:natural%3Dtree/List_of_Genus,_Leaf_cycle,_Leaf_type). `deciduous` and `semi_deciduous` are considered equivalent, as are `evergreen` and `semi_evergreen`; `palm` counts as `broadleaved` and `leafless` is not compared.
* `leaf_type=*` other than `broadleaved`, `needleleaved`, `leafless`, `palm`
* `leaf_cycle=*` other than `deciduous`, `semi_deciduous`, `evergreen`, `semi_evergreen`
* `denotation=*` rare or invalid. Each part of a multiple value (`natural_monument;landmark`) is checked on its own, against the number of uses on nodes according to [Taginfo](https://taginfo.openstreetmap.org/keys/denotation#values). It is reported if:
  * it has an invalid format: characters other than lowercase letters, digits and `_` (for example `=`, `:`, `/`, spaces, uppercase) or it does not start with a letter;
  * it is a common value (at least 1000 uses) with other text attached without `_`, for example `urbanheidialhoranta` or `single1953`;
  * it is used at most 10 times.

  The thresholds are at the top of `scripts/fetch_trees.py` (`DEN_RARE_MAX`, `DEN_COMMON_MIN`). The format check always applies; the other two require `scripts/denotation_values.json`.

The list-based checks require `scripts/species_list.json`, `scripts/genus_list.json` and `scripts/denotation_values.json`, which are generated by the "Update reference lists" Action; if the files are missing, the checks are not run.

If `circumference=*` is missing but `diameter=*` is present, the circumference is derived as π × diameter. As the [wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:diameter) states, `diameter` without a unit is in millimetres (unlike `circumference` and `diameter_crown`, in metres); written units (`mm`, `cm`, `m`, `ft`, `in`) are respected. Values without a unit below 10 are not used (see the check above). The derived circumference goes through the same checks and estimates; if both tags are present, `diameter` is ignored (but the checks on the format of numeric values still apply to `diameter` too).

The checks are computed by `scripts/fetch_trees.py` and saved in the `issues` field of `data/regions/<region>.geojson.gz`.

## Hints

Besides errors, the tree card can show hints: information that could improve the data, not errors to fix. They appear after the errors, preceded by ⓘ, in grey and not bold; they do not colour the tree on the map, are not in the errors menu and do not count in the ranking. Only in the QA tool, not in the monumental trees map. This is also where checks that would give too many false positives as errors live.

* No `species` or `genus`, but the tree's Wikidata item (`wikidata=*`) indicates the species ([P10241](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P10241)): `species` could be added with that name, after a survey on the ground
* `species:wikidata=*` (a taxon) without `species`: `species` could be added with the item's scientific name (P225)
* `species` in the wiki species list, without `species:wikidata`: the QID from the list could be added
* `species:wikipedia=*` without `species:wikidata` (when the list does not already give the QID, see above): `species:wikidata` could be added with the QID of the item linked to the Wikipedia article
* `species:wikipedia=*` without `species` (and without the two hints on `species` above): `species` could be added with the scientific name (P225) of the item linked to the article
* For both, the article must have a Wikidata item that is a species (a taxon, not of genus rank or higher). Not the other way round: `species:wikidata` without `species:wikipedia` is not reported
* `species:it=*` with a lowercase initial (each value, if there are several separated by `;`): on OSM 98% of the values have a capital (for example `Platano comune`), so a capital is suggested. It is a hint and not an error because it is not a rule written on the wiki
* `ref:masaf=*` without `masaf:criteria`: the criteria that the MASAF list gives for that code could be added (from `scripts/masaf_trees.json`)
* Only in Italian regions: a tree without `denotation=natural_monument` or `landmark` and without `ref:masaf`, with a circumference (from `circumference`, or from `diameter`) at least equal to the MASAF indicative threshold for its species: it could be a monumental tree. The thresholds are the MASAF "minimum indicative circumferences for the size criterion" (January 2020), transcribed in `scripts/masaf_thresholds.json`, only for the species in the table (for Betula, which has 200 or 300 cm, 300 is used)
* `source=*` of a tree import listed in `scripts/imports.json` ([issue #2](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/2)): "Tree from an undocumented import" for imports without documentation, "Imported tree" with the link to the wiki page for documented ones. The comparison ignores case, surrounding spaces and the trailing slash; with several values separated by `;` one is enough
* `genus=*` of a genus with a single living species (the others are only fossils), without `species`: `species` could be added with that species (for example `genus=Ginkgo` → `species=Ginkgo biloba`). The genera are in a hand-written list (`MONOTYPIC` in `index.html`): Ginkgo, Metasequoia, Sequoiadendron, Sequoia, Cryptomeria, Sciadopitys, Glyptostrobus, Platycladus, Thujopsis, Pseudolarix, Wollemia, Fitzroya, Davidia, Idesia, Eucommia, Tetracentron, Trochodendron, Oxydendrum, Franklinia, Umbellularia. For `genus=Sequoia` in Italy, French-speaking and English-speaking countries (from the region's ISO code: IT, FR, BE, LU, CH, MC, GB, IE, US, CA, AU, NZ) a second hint adds that there the common name "sequoia" is often used for the giant sequoia (*Sequoiadendron giganteum*) too, so the tree should be checked
* `leaf_type` or `leaf_cycle` missing, with the species or genus in the wiki lists: the values from the lists could be added (wrong ones are already errors)
* `wikidata=*` without `denotation=natural_monument` or `landmark`: a tree with a Wikidata item is notable, and could have one of the two (it does not appear if an error already says so, for example protection on Wikidata)
* `species=Ginkgo biloba` (or `genus=Ginkgo` without `species`) without `sex=*`, only from April to November (October to May in the southern hemisphere, from the tree's latitude): `sex=female` could be added if the tree is female, with a note on when and how to check ([issue #25](https://github.com/ivanbranco/tree-osm-qa/issues/25)). The words "foul-smelling seeds" are underlined with a dashed line: a click or tap opens a photo of the seeds ([File:Gingko biloba seeds.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gingko_biloba_seeds.jpg)) with the Commons credits, as for the card photos (loaded only when opened); a second click, a click elsewhere or Esc closes it

The first nine are computed in `hint_props` of `scripts/fetch_trees.py` (properties `hint_sp`, `hint_sn`, `hint_sw`, `hint_wp`, `hint_wn`, `hint_it`, `hint_mc`, `hint_ms`, `hint_imp_u` / `hint_imp_d`), the last four in the page (`hints` in `index.html`) from the tree's data.
